Rise Up Kids helps children ages 5 and up learn English through research based natural language immersion. English is understood, lived, and expressed with confidence, without translation and without pressure.

Rise Up Kids is an ecosystem of immersive and meaningful experiences for families and schools. Our intentional and progressive structure brings together technology, interactive activities, and real teachers so children move from understanding to speaking with joy and consistency.

WHO CONTROLS THE EXPERIENCE
Children do not create or control accounts. Access is managed by grown ups:

• Parents and guardians for family learning
• Schools, admins, and teachers for school programs

These adults create child profiles, supervise use, and control settings. Children use the learning experience under that adult managed account.

WHAT CHILDREN EXPERIENCE
• Research based natural language learning through meaningful context
• Videos with real kids that teach English in context
• Interactive books and storytime with visual support
• StarCam missions that turn everyday objects into English practice
• Guided activities that build comprehension and speaking
• Kids Wall on Android and Web, where children can share photos of the activities they are doing as they experience the language
• Stars, progress, and a learning journey children want to continue
• New content added regularly

HOW CHILDREN LEARN
First, the child understands. Then they start to communicate. Learning follows how children naturally acquire language: through context, interaction, and meaningful repetition, not memorization alone. Children are encouraged to use English from early on, with curiosity, consistency, and growing autonomy.

KIDS WALL
Kids Wall lets children share the activities they are doing as they experience the language. Photos may show projects, materials, and learning moments. Photos of faces and body parts are not allowed. Kids Wall is available on the Android app and Web only. It is not available on iOS. Where Kids Wall is available, posts are reviewed by our team before they can appear to other families, and parents or school adults can allow or block posting for each child.

FOR PARENTS, SCHOOLS, ADMINS, AND TEACHERS
Whether your child learns at home or at school, a responsible adult stays in control. Adults manage profiles, follow progress, and supervise features. Parents can manage family child profiles from the app or parent web dashboard. Schools, admins, and teachers manage learning in their school programs according to their roles.

PRIVACY AND CHILD SAFETY
We design the Service for families and schools and follow COPPA principles for children's privacy. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use children's personal information for third party behavioral advertising.

• Adult managed accounts control all child profiles
• Children do not provide email addresses or passwords
• StarCam may use the camera to complete learning missions. Photos used for object recognition are processed to finish the activity and are not kept after processing
• Speaking activities may use the microphone for learning practice
• Kids Wall photo sharing is Android and Web only, with activity focused photos, no faces or body parts, and adult moderation controls
